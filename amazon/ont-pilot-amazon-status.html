How the pilot works

Sites
ONT42 and ONT43, Ontario, CA
Vehicles
Middle-mile tractors, Class 7 and 8 first. SVT services Class 1 to 8.
Work requests
Through Reach, connected to Relay Garage. SVT dispatchers use the Reach web portal; technicians use the Reach mobile app.
Site access
Under DTNA's Avetta clearance. Safety vest, gate code by email.
Invoicing
Through DTNA / Corcentric
Coverage
SVT Corona / Inland Empire

How we'll measure it

To agree with Amazon before go-live.

  • Median days to complete a repair, against today's baseline (ONT42 10.9 days, ONT43 11.0 days)
  • Share of repairs open more than 7 and more than 14 days
  • Tractors down at any time
