Method

How this hub runs the full project cycle, and the rules it applies.

Operating rhythm

  • Daily (10 min): open Today. Do the top card in each lane: one for registration, one for station openings, one for depth.
  • After every touch (under 2 min): update the DSP's stage, next action and last touch. Add an evidence entry for anything you could be asked to prove later.
  • Friday (15 min): clear the stale list. Any deal untouched for 30 days gets revived with a dated action or marked Lost.
  • Monthly, 1st: send Michael a one-screen update covering registration status, stations opened, deals by stage and what's blocked. Log it as evidence.
  • Within 24 hours of any objective met: send the confirmation email to Michael with the date, unit count, tier and amount, attach the proof, and log it.

Rules the hub applies

  • Sequence: Registration (#3) comes first because it is the foundational unlock. For DSPs, go wide before deep. Three first conversions are worth $15,000 and open three stations. One first plus four additional at one station is worth $13,000.
  • First DSP tiers: 75+ units $5,000 · 50–74 $4,000 · 25–49 $3,000 · 1–24 $1,000.
  • Additional DSP tiers (max 4 per station): 50+ $2,000 · 25–49 $1,500 · 10–25 $1,000 · 1–9 $0. At exactly 25 units the tiers overlap, so get the reading in writing.
  • "Units" is undefined in the agreement. Confirm with SVT that it means vehicles before the first PMA signs.
  • Earning gate: objectives must be completed during the term. The Initial Term ends Jan 31, 2027. A deal at 95% on that date pays $0.
  • Stage ladder: Identified → Qualified → Engaged → Proposed → Verbal → Signed → Verified → Paid (plus Lost).
