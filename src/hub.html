Method

How this hub runs the full project cycle, and the rules it applies.

Operating rhythm

  • Daily (10 min): open Today. Do the top card in each lane: Vendor Approval first, then Middle Mile, then Last Mile.
  • After every Amazon touch (under 2 min): update the step or site, then add an evidence entry for anything you might need to prove later (who said what, in writing or in a meeting).
  • Friday (15 min): clear the Waiting list. Chase or re-date anything older than 7 days.
  • Monthly, 1st: send Michael a one-screen update covering approval status, site stages, blockers and decisions needed. Log it as evidence.
  • Within 24 hours of Amazon's written confirmation: forward it to Michael with the date and the Exhibit A #3 amount, and log it.

Rules the hub applies

  • Scope: Amazon Vendor Approval and Amazon Station Conversions only. No DSP outreach, lead lists or routing workflows.
  • Trigger wording (Exhibit A #3): "written confirmation from Amazon that SVT has been approved as an active, registered vendor." Element, Supplier Connect, DTNA clearance and "selectable in Relay Garage" don't count on their own.
  • Own name: the confirmation must name SVT FLEET LLC, not SVT working under DTNA's or Velocity's approval.
  • Never claim it early: don't call SVT "Amazon approved" in any message until the confirmation exists.
  • Station conversions and Exhibit A: Middle Mile pilot work sits under Exhibit A's unpriced "other named opportunities" line. Agree the incentive with Michael in writing before more pilot work.
  • Earning gate: objectives must be completed during the term. The Initial Term ends Jan 31, 2027.
