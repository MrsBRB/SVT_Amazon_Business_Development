Overview

What the pilot is, how work flows, and the two sites.

How it works

Customer
Amazon Middle Mile, AFP/APP tractor yards (owner: Evan Christodoulides)
Work requests
Through the Reach portal and mobile app. Reach connects to Amazon's Relay Garage, so the pilot needs no repair-order system integration.
Reach account
New SVT service-provider account. $3 per service event, paid by SVT. $30 setup fee, credited after 10 events.
Site access
On-site entry under DTNA's Avetta clearance. Safety vest required, no badge or orientation, gate code by email.
Approvals
Pre-authorization about $1,000 per OEM (to confirm for Reach).
Invoicing
Through DTNA / Corcentric (process and SVT owner to confirm).
Go-live

Success measures (to agree with Amazon)

  • Median days to complete a repair, against the ONT42 baseline of 10.9 days
  • Share of repairs open more than 7 and more than 14 days
  • Tractors down at any time

Next sites if the pilot works: EWR40 (New Jersey), then the EWR45 / PHL40 / PHL42 corridor. Amazon has also asked whether other ONT sites should join.
